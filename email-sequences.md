# Tenfold Ads — Email Sequences

Three sequences: **nurture** (leads from the landing page and cold calls), **onboarding** (after a church says yes), and **re-engagement** (leads gone quiet). Written to be loaded into any email tool.

**Merge tags used:** `{{FirstName}}` `{{ChurchName}}` `{{City}}` — replace with your tool's syntax (Mailchimp `*|FNAME|*`, etc.)

**Sending rules:**
- Send from a person ("Ben at Tenfold Ads"), never from "info@"
- One CTA per email. Plain formatting — this audience reads email in Outlook between meetings
- Always include your physical mailing address + unsubscribe link in the footer (CAN-SPAM)
- Site links assume your live domain; replace `tenfoldads.com` placeholders throughout

## Footnotes for all sequences

- **The 5% rule** appears in nurture emails 1.1, 1.2, and 1.5 deliberately — it is the honest spine of the offer. Never soften it; it's the reason you exist.
- **Timeline language** ("usually days, occasionally a couple of weeks") is intentionally hedged. Google's verification has no SLA. Never promise a date.
- **Report email (2.7)** is a template — the bracketed fields get written fresh each month per client. Keep it honest, keep it one page.
- **Unsubscribes:** these are relationship emails, not blasts — but include the link anyway. A pastor who can't easily leave will never come back.
