# Tenfold Ads — Facebook Ad Pack

Ready-to-use Facebook/Instagram ad creative for the Tenfold Ads church Google Ad Grant service.

**Landing page:** https://bensblueprints.github.io/tenfold-ads-landing/

## Messaging rules

- Say **up to $10,000/month in search ad credit**, not guaranteed cash or guaranteed approval.
- Google determines eligibility and approval.
- The grant is ad credit, not cash.
- Ongoing requirements include Google's 5% account-level click-through-rate rule.
- Tenfold Ads charges $1,000/month for application help, campaign build, and ongoing management.
- The account and data stay in the church's name.
- Use a free 15-minute eligibility check as the main call to action.

## Flagship 30-second video — “Church Near Me”

Use captions throughout; most people watch without sound.

- **0–4 sec** — *Visual: someone types “church near me” into Google.*

### Targeting notes

- **Ad set 1:** US, roughly 30–65+, church leadership job titles and church/ministry interests available in the Meta account.
- **Ad set 2:** Broad US audience with the same creative; let the “church near me” message self-select the audience.
- Retarget landing-page visitors and people who watch at least 50% of the video with the **5% rule** message.
- Install the **Meta Pixel and a Lead event** before optimizing the campaign for leads.
- Do not upload scraped church emails as a Custom Audience unless permission to use them is documented; build lookalikes from opt-in leads instead.

## Testing plan

Keep the audience constant while comparing framings so the winning message is identifiable:

- **Money:** Ads 1 and 6
- **Personal offer:** Ad 2
- **Small-church impact:** Ad 4
- **Search intent:** Ad 8
- **Compliance:** Ad 9
- **Board approval:** Ad 10

Initial launch recommendation:

- Run **Ads 1, 2, and 4** to cold audiences.
- Use **Ads 3 and 5** to retarget video viewers and landing-page visitors who did not submit the form.
- Test the flagship video against the static versions with the same audience and placement settings.
- Optimize for leads only after the Meta Pixel and Lead event are installed and firing correctly.
