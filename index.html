The fine print

Why churches lose the grant

The gift is generous. The condition is strict. This is the part worth understanding.

Google's one hard rule: at least 5 of every 100 people who see your ad must click it — every month. Slip under that line two months in a row, and Google can cancel the grant entirely.

Writing church ads that people actually click, week after week, is a craft. Most congregations don't have a Google Ads specialist in the pews. So grants go unclaimed — or get quietly taken back.

Honest answers

Questions a good steward would ask

Is this real money?+

It's real ad spend, not cash in the bank. Google credits your church's account with up to $10,000 every month, and your ads run in the same search results, with the same power, as any paid campaign.

The credit renews monthly, as long as your church stays qualified — which is the part we handle.

Do churches really qualify?+

Yes. Churches have been eligible for the Google Ad Grant since the program began. If your church holds 501(c)(3) status and has a live website, you're likely eligible. We'll confirm the details on the call before you commit to anything.

What happens if our click-through rate falls below 5%?+

Google gives you two months of grace before they step in — and making sure that never happens is our whole job. We monitor weekly, retire ads that tire, and refresh copy so your account stays comfortably above the line. You'll see it in every monthly report.

Whose name is on the account?+

Yours. The grant, the ads account, and every click of data belong to your church — not to us. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you, and your congregation keeps the benefit of everything we built.

What would we have to do?+

One call to begin. Ten minutes to approve the ad copy. Then welcome the people who walk through the door. The paperwork, the keywords, the bidding, the weekly vigilance over the 5% line — that's ours.

Why "Tenfold"?+

From the parable of the talents — faithful over a little, trusted over much. And from arithmetic: every dollar your church gives us becomes ten dollars telling your city about Jesus.
