What we need from you

Three clicks and one call

Only your church can do these — the accounts must belong to you, in your name. Each one takes minutes, and none of them can break anything.

01

Create your church's Google account

You ≈ 10 minutes one-time setup

This account becomes the master key: the grant, the ads, and every click of data will belong to your church, in your name — never to us. Use a dedicated account for the church, not a personal one.

If your church has its own email domain (like office@yourchurch.org), Google can attach an account to that address — choose "Use my current email address instead" during signup. Otherwise, a new Gmail address like yourchurchads@gmail.com works just as well.

Create your Google account opens Google in a new tab
Tip: record the password somewhere safe, and make sure a second staff member can recover it. This account outlives our work together — it's yours.
02

Claim your Google for Nonprofits profile

You ≈ 5 minutes EIN required

Sign in at the link below with the account you just created, then follow "Get started" to verify your church's eligibility. You'll need your EIN, and your church's legal name and address exactly as the IRS has them — a mismatch there is the only thing that ever slows this down.

Google's verification partner confirms your nonprofit status, usually within a few days. This is the one step no one can do for you — it's their way of making sure the account really belongs to your church.

Google for Nonprofits sign in with your new church account
Tip: Google sometimes asks for your 501(c)(3) determination letter. A photo or scan of it resolves the request the same day — having it ready now never hurts.
03

Enroll in the Google Ad Grant

You ≈ 5 minutes after your profile is active

Once your Google for Nonprofits status shows active, enroll in the Ad Grant — from your dashboard's products list, or the link below. Don't create a Google Ads account yourself beforehand — Google provisions the grant account for you when your enrollment is approved, and a pre-made account just complicates the paperwork.

The moment your enrollment is approved, your account can spend up to $10,000 a month in search advertising — the budget renews every month you stay qualified.

Google Ad Grants enroll once your profile is active
04

Add us to the account — together, on a call

You + us ≈ 15 minutes we'll schedule it

On our setup call, you'll invite us to manage the ads — a couple of clicks inside Google Ads. The account stays yours; we just get keys to drive. From this moment on, the applications, the keywords, the bidding, and the weekly watch over the 5% line are ours.

Book your setup call or just reply to your welcome email

Meanwhile, on our side of the fence

While you click, we're already at work

  • Preparing your campaigns — the searches your city makes, and the words that win the click.
  • Drafting your ad copy — you'll approve it in about ten minutes, before anything goes live.
  • Watching for Google's verification emails — and nudging them along so nothing sits idle.
  • Setting up your tracking — so the monthly report shows real people finding your church, not vanity numbers.

Have these within reach

✓

Your church's EIN — the tax ID number. It's on any giving statement or IRS letter.

✓

Legal name & mailing address — exactly as the IRS has them. This is where mismatches happen.

✓

✓

Twenty minutes, all told. The other few weeks of work are ours.
